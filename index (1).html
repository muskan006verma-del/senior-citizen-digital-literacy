About this website

This website is a community project by students of the Department of Information Technology, Priyadarshini College of Engineering, Nagpur. It helps senior citizens use their smartphones with confidence.

Learn step by step

Tap a lesson to open it. Read slowly. Try it on your own phone.

Make a phone call
  1. Tap the green Phone icon.
  2. Tap Contacts and pick the name, or type the number on the keypad.
  3. Tap the green call button.
  4. Tap the red button to end the call.
Send a WhatsApp message
  1. Open WhatsApp (green icon).
  2. Tap the chat of the person you want.
  3. Tap the box at the bottom and type your message.
  4. Tap the green arrow to send.
Make a video call
  1. Open the chat of your family member in WhatsApp.
  2. Tap the small camera icon at the top.
  3. Wait for them to answer. Hold the phone at face level.
  4. Tap the red button to end.
Pay safely with UPI
  1. Open your payment app (like Google Pay, PhonePe or BHIM).
  2. Tap Scan, and scan the shop's QR code.
  3. Type the amount and check the shop name.
  4. Enter your PIN only to send money. Never share it.
  5. Wait for the green success message before you leave.
Make the text bigger on your phone
  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Display, then Font size.
  3. Slide to a size you like.

Stay safe from fraud

Answer the question. Tap one choice.
